Hull · Gatineau · Federation of Canadian Municipalities

Three decades in service of his city.

City councillor, mayor of Hull, first mayor of the new City of Gatineau.

Yves Ducharme led Hull for nine years, then guided the merger that created the City of Gatineau. In 2003 he presided over the Federation of Canadian Municipalities. In 2026 he returns to the public conversation with a proposal to Finance Canada.

Career

Verified milestones
1958

Born in Montréal

Born May 29, 1958.

1986 – 1992

Hull city councillor

He represents the Mont-Bleu district.

1992 – 2001

Mayor of Hull

Elected mayor on December 6, 1992, he serves until December 31, 2001.

2002 – 2005

First mayor of the City of Gatineau

Elected in November 2001, he takes office on January 1, 2002, leading the city formed by the merger of Hull, Gatineau, Aylmer, Buckingham and Masson-Angers.

2003

66th president of the FCM

He presides over the Federation of Canadian Municipalities.

2006

Order of the Aztec Eagle

A simple idea, a simple refund

With Joseph Soares · September 28, 2026

Yves Ducharme presents his proposal to Finance Canada and explains why simplicity makes the difference. The interview is in French.
