A tax credit for travelling in Canada

Mr. Ducharme has filed a proposal with Finance Canada for a tax credit that would encourage Canadians to travel in their own country. He explains the reasoning, and the conditions for it to work, in the first interview episode of Corridor Intelligence, Joseph Soares’s analysis program. The interview is in French.

The idea is not new: he had it adopted unanimously by the FCM’s general assembly during his presidency.

“It’s a good old new idea that carries even more weight now, given the tariff war with our neighbours to the south.”Yves Ducharme

The proposal

The entire stay must take place in a province other than one’s own or the one next door, over a five-year program.

Two conditions

An amount large enough to change a destination, and absolute simplicity.

“If it’s too complicated to get your refund, people will give up. It has to be simple.”Yves Ducharme

The conversation also covers how Paul Martin freed municipalities from the GST in a single federal decision, and why cities have disappeared from Canadian political discourse. “It’s as if we don’t exist,” the former mayor says.

Transparency: Mr. Ducharme and Mr. Soares each filed their own submission to Finance Canada’s pre-budget consultations.
